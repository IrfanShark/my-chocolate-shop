Chocolatier's Tasting Tips

  1. Portion Guide: One standard chocolate square weighs approximately 10 grams.
  2. Savor Slowly: Let the piece melt on your tongue rather than chewing to release the full cocoa butter aroma.
  3. Daily Balance: 2 to 3 pieces of 70% dark chocolate provide antioxidants without excess sugar.
